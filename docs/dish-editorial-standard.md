# Dish editorial publication contract

Every public dish requires two substantive, independently edited articles: at least
5,000 words in Hebrew and at least 5,000 words in English. The English edition is a
localisation, not mechanical sentence-by-sentence translation.

Publication and indexation are blocked until each language record contains:

- eight credible source URLs, including at least two archival, academic,
  governmental, museum, library or other primary/authoritative sources;
- notes separating established history from community/family tradition and disputed
  claims;
- a recorded kitchen test with date, ID, recipe version, yield, weights,
  temperatures, timing, ingredients and instructions;
- named/date-stamped kitchen and allergen reviews;
- an allergen record matching the tested recipe;
- public-image approval;
- Hebrew and English editor names, editorial date and originality review;
- qualified nutrition review whenever health language is present.

Operational BOM, supplier, cost, stock, loss and internal safety records remain in
Complete99 OS. Visitors never receive editorial state, missing-data, delivery-phase
or implementation language. An unready fact is omitted; an unready dish remains
private.

The admin publication panel reports every failed gate. WordPress also forces a
publish/future request back to private draft status, returns a public 404 for any
unready dish, adds `noindex`, and withholds Recipe schema.
