# Complete99 Syrian Aleppo public vertical source and claim ledger

Status: internal operating record for the noindex public preview

Review date: 2026-08-08

Public audience: hungry readers, culinary students, chefs, restaurant operators and pantry shoppers

This ledger governs the first Syrian consumer journey: Syrian cuisine to Aleppo, the Aleppine kibbeh family, bulgur, lamb and beef, hydration, fully cooked kibbeh methods and Aleppan Jewish foodways. It is not public-facing source copy, a tested recipe, a raw-kibbeh process, a supplier agreement, a price approval, a stock promise, a kosher certificate or a food-safety plan.

## Release boundary

The release may expose exactly seven new bilingual owners beside the existing Syrian cuisine root. Every owner remains `noindex_until_longform_review`, `search_index=false` and outside the sitemap. `dish-kibbeh-meshwiyyeh` remains private with `culinary_test_status=pending`; it has no public route, link, Recipe schema or availability claim.

Only `ingredient-syrian-bulgur` may continue to the independently verified WooCommerce identity `product-bulgur-fine-500g`. WooCommerce remains the sole authority for the exact product name, pack size, price, inventory and cart action. No broad meat-family page may resolve to an individual beef or lamb SKU.
