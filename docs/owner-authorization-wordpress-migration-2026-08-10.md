## Authorized outcome

Complete99 is to operate as one WordPress-owned system that does not require a
person to sign in to ChatGPT or keep an agent conversation open. The migration
scope includes:

- the bilingual public site and its server-rendered theme experience;
- the Culinary Science Museum, dishes, ingredients, techniques, equipment,
  traditions, visual assets and their sourced relationships;
- the international pantry and exact WooCommerce product relationships;
- the authenticated private operating application, its modules, roles,
  storage, APIs, audit history and approval queues;
- editorial research, bilingual review, rights review, food-safety review and
  publication decisions;
- deterministic build, protected Git/CI release, rollback, cache, health and
  live acceptance evidence.

The WordPress plugin is the versioned application boundary. Public discovery
is rendered by WordPress. Private operating functions are role-scoped inside
WordPress and exposed only through authenticated, capability-checked WordPress
interfaces and APIs. WooCommerce remains the commerce engine. Theme behavior
required for the application is delivered by the plugin through WordPress
hooks and versioned assets so that it is not dependent on manual theme edits.

## Authority granted

The owner authorizes the implementation team to inspect and change the scoped
Complete99 repository, create and update WordPress plugin code and data,
produce deterministic artifacts, commit and push reviewed changes, use the
protected-main CI workflow, and perform a controlled WordPress deployment with
backup, locking, stabilization, independent verification and cleanup.

The owner also authorizes a complete visibility investigation covering Google
search observations, robots.txt, sitemaps, HTTP and HTML robots directives,
canonicals, hreflang, structured data, internal links, WordPress search
visibility and Search Console evidence when an authenticated property is
available.

## Boundaries that the authorization does not fabricate

This authorization is operational authority. It does not fabricate a
credential, supplier relationship, payment-provider activation, certification,
food-safety conclusion, tested recipe, legal conclusion, image right, stock
fact, customer claim or provider receipt.

Those facts may be migrated and reviewed inside WordPress, but they become
public or operational only when their exact evidence and accountable decision
are attached to the exact versioned record. Until then they remain private,
editorial, disabled or noindex according to their existing truth state.

The existing twelve Syrian and Japanese publication candidates may move
through the WordPress approval workflow under this authorization. Promotion
still binds the exact bilingual content and exact asset receipts so that a
broad authorization cannot accidentally approve altered bytes or a different
claim later.

## Release rules retained

- No production write bypasses protected main, required CI or exact-artifact
  verification.
- No privileged temporary route remains installed after a deployment.
- Every deployment keeps a scoped backup, lock, stabilization checkpoint,
  rollback path, cache purge and independent live proof.
- Secrets stay outside Git and are never printed into logs or artifacts.
- Public, private, editorial and commerce states remain explicit and
  fail-closed.
- Google indexability is decided per canonical owner page; private/editorial
  application surfaces are never made crawlable merely to increase visibility.

## Initial execution order

4. Migrate the remaining modules in coherent vertical slices and connect their
   shared identity graph.
5. Correct public indexability only for approved canonical discovery pages.
6. Release through protected main, verify in real Chrome and record rollback
